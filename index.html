What changed

Campaign results, and problems found by comparing what reports said with what actually happened.

  • 55% higherqualified-lead rate at similar CPL

    Lead form analysis

    Matched form answers to CRM stages. The same setup showed an estimated 42% lower cost per sale. Ad sets differed, so I treated this as strong but not final evidence.

  • 37% lowercost per lead

    Lead form and creative testing

    Combined form optimisation, creative A/B tests and controlled budget pacing. Conversion also rose from 0.75% to 1.30% on Meta and 0.65% to 1.5% on Google Search.

  • 55% of ad spendwas missing from reports

    Hidden channel cost

    Messaging, SMS and automation costs sat outside ad reporting. Joining them changed how every efficiency number should be read.

  • 122s to 1sfirst reply time

    Speed to lead

    Every reply took exactly 122 seconds. Constant delay pointed to a routing fault, so the fix was in the wiring, not server capacity.

  • 736 to 487send attempts vs billed conversations

    Spend reporting fix

    Reports treated attempts as cost. Separating attempts, deliveries and billed conversations removed a 34% gap.
